#  Email Draft to Microscopy Researchers to explore their interest in IIIF-based image aggregation and comparison

Dear [Name],

I hope you are doing well.

I am currently exploring ways to improve how microscopy image datasets can be organized, compared, and reused within 4TU.ResearchData, and I would greatly value your perspective as someone working with this type of data.

At the moment, high-resolution images in the repository can be accessed individually, but I am investigating whether we could enhance their interoperability by using the International Image Interoperability Framework (IIIF). While IIIF has mainly been adopted in digital collections, we are considering whether it could also support scientific use cases.

One idea we are exploring is the following:

* Visualize the context of each image datasets by aggregating metadata of categories, related publications (resource_title and resource_doi) and derived from, time coverage , geolocation(custom_fields) in the metadata of the manifest (so it is shown in the viewer, so far is not shown with the current implementation of the manifest).
* Import annotations per image in the dataset, for example, to show the region of interest in the image or to add comments on the image.




Before developing this further, I would like to understand whether such functionality would actually be useful in your research practice.

In particular, I would be interested in your thoughts on:

* Whether you ever need to visually compare images across experiments or datasets.
* Whether browsing images grouped by author, topic, or condition would be helpful.
* Whether having publication and provenance metadata directly visible in an image viewer would add value.
* Any current challenges you experience when sharing or reusing microscopy images.

If you would be willing to share your perspective (either via email or a short meeting), it would greatly help us assess whether this direction is meaningful for researchers.

Thank you very much for your time, and I look forward to hearing your thoughts.

Best regards,

[Your Name]
